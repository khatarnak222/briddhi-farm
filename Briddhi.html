🌿 About Briddhi Farm

Briddhi Farm is a thoughtfully developed aquaculture and agri-based destination near Guwahati focused on delivering a premium recreational angling experience.

The farm blends scientific fishery management with a peaceful natural landscape, creating the perfect environment for both serious anglers and weekend visitors.

Our long-term vision is to build one of Assam’s most trusted and well-managed private angling destinations.
